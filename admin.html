      Live sync

      Keep every screen in the room together. One draw board runs the draw; the others — an LED wall, a lobby screen, a livestream PC — spin and reveal the same winner at the same moment, and can follow it to the welcome and prize screens.

      Account

      Credentials are stored as a salted PBKDF2 hash inside appsettings.json, which is never served to a browser.

      Change sign-in details

      Credentials are pinned by the ADMIN_USERNAME and ADMIN_PASSWORD environment variables, so they cannot be changed here.
